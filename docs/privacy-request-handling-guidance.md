# Privacy request handling guidance

Date: 31 August 2026

`/privacy/requests` is information only. It does not submit, store or promise automated handling of personal data. Before production activation, the controller must configure an approved contact route and operating procedure.

UK requests may be verbal or written. The controller should explain applicable access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, consent-withdrawal and complaint rights; respond normally within one calendar month; and use a lawful extension only for complex or multiple requests with timely explanation. Identity checks must be proportionate. Anonymous ownership credentials may help verify authority for a result or challenge, but stored hashes are not bearer credentials and the service may be unable to identify a person from anonymous data alone.

Users should not be asked to send payment-card details, passwords or unnecessary identity documents. The route must not claim that a public deletion API exists. Complaints may be made to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office, but controller identity, contact wording and any case-specific legal position require owner and professional approval.
