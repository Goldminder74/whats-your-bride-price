# Children and intended audience decision record

Date: 31 August 2026

The product is intended primarily for adults and people above the applicable age of digital consent. It is not directed at children under 13 in the UK and must not target children below the applicable digital-consent age.

Prompt 19 does not invent a 13+, 16+ or 18+ product minimum, add an age gate, collect dates of birth, infer age, profile children, request parental identity documents or introduce child-directed marketing. The quiz must not be represented as designed for young children.

The final numeric minimum audience, Children’s Code applicability and any associated safeguards require explicit owner and appropriate professional approval before production activation. Until then, the legal routes remain draft, unapproved and excluded from indexing.
