# Data retention schedule

Updated: 8 October 2026

Status: owner-approved policy implemented locally; separate activation authorisation and professional legal/privacy review remain required.

The canonical row data is `app/retentionSchedule.ts` and `/privacy/retention` renders it. Each row states purpose, location, retention trigger, maximum period, expiry behavior, deletion or anonymisation behavior, user control and activation state.

The implemented result rule is authoritative: completed attempts, answers and results expire exactly 90 days after authoritative quiz completion. Publication has no effect on `expires_at`; it does not extend, restart or remove expiry. Unpublication, deletion or another valid lifecycle action can make a result unavailable sooner, and copies or caches outside the service are not controlled by the application.

Challenges remain capped at 30 days or the source resultâ€™s remaining life, whichever is earlier. Accepted challenge play is also bounded by its own 24-hour authority. Raw version-1 referral, share and analytics events cannot exceed 30 days. Analytics choices persist for 180 days and analytics sessions for at most 24 hours. Pending Royal Reveal authority lasts 30 minutes; the former 400-day webhook technical expiry cannot remove unresolved, held or financially required evidence; the approved financial rules below take precedence. Ordinary Royal Reveal regeneration requires an active result, at most 90 days from completion; a protected return may finish a verified payment already in progress. Downloaded files remain device-controlled.

Daily challenges and streaks remain off by default. The approved product rule creates a streak only after an authoritative official daily completion and sets `expires_at` to exactly 180 days later. Each later valid official completion resets that deadline; practice, page visits, failures, retries, analytics, sharing and ordinary quiz play do not. A missed UTC day resets the current count while retaining the best count until the record expires. At expiry the streak is unavailable immediately, the bounded operation must remove it within seven days, and a returning player begins a new streak without reconnecting the former record. Clear my streak data makes the record unavailable immediately and permanently removes it within seven days; the current repository removes it in the same atomic request. Ownership hashes are functional only and must never be reused for analytics, marketing or cross-site tracking. Identifiable deletion logs are prohibited except where strictly required for security and operational integrity.

Daily recovery lasts only until the next UTC boundary, successful completion or a relevant clear control. Party features remain inactive and have no approved production retention. The Prompt 22 rules, migration and routes are local source only: production activation still requires documented privacy and legal review, an approved D1 binding, an approved server secret and separate deployment and migration approval.

Cowrie bonuses expire exactly 180 days after their authoritative award. Purchased Cowries do not expire while an active wallet remains available. Expiry appends a bounded, idempotent ledger adjustment and never rewrites history; an old spent bonus cannot consume a newer valid credit. Clear freezes wallet access immediately and preserves its balance, ledger and free-play counter for protected support handling. The owner approved the financial/support policy below on 5 October 2026; the seven-day non-financial purge rule does not apply to financial records. Safe implementation and professional review remain required before production activation.

## Implemented locally — 8 October 2026

Owner approval is distinct from professional legal sign-off. The final closure definition is an explicit authoritative operator action only after all pending payments/reversals, purchased value (used or verified-refunded), unresolved claims/holds and wallet/order/ledger reconciliation are resolved. Clearing local data, freezing, inactivity and expiry are not closure. Purchased value is never forfeited. The closure timestamp is immutable and starts the approved 30-day wallet minimisation clock; recovery cannot reopen it.

The single additive migration `0012_retention_authority.sql` stores durable holds, minimal cases, verified settlement authority, closures, minimisation and restore suppression. Existing migrations 0000–0011 and accounting/ledger protections remain intact. The only order-authority exception irreversibly removes owner linkage after the approved financial period and dependency checks; it cannot alter amount, target or allocation identity. Retained financial lineage is minimised necessary proof, not reusable customer access. Non-financial expiry/erasure drains bounded answer/completion rows and anonymises identifying fields without reviving access; case and financial purge respects scoped holds.

[Private operations and restore instructions](retention-operations.md) document authenticated target/action approvals, no public operator route, bounded repeat-safe maintenance, annual necessity review and operational export custody. Restores require independent suppression/hold/case/closure evidence, preserved original clocks and financial reconciliation. A matching separately installed runtime receipt is required before a data/payment profile or job can run. An unannounced privileged provider restore cannot be detected by a DB flag alone; disabling access before restore and preserving independent authority are mandatory.

Official compatibility checks on 8 October 2026: [HMRC](https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-records/how-long-to-keep-your-records) supports the five-year filing-deadline minimum; the implementation uses Europe/London tax-year boundaries. [ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/right-to-erasure/) permits necessary legal obligation/claim exceptions and backup data beyond ordinary use. [D1 Time Travel](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/reference/time-travel/) documents seven-day Free/30-day Paid recovery windows, both within the approved maximum. [D1 foreign keys](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/sql-api/foreign-keys/) supports transactional deferral; local D1 tests verify the actual migration and purge. These are documented requirements and local checks, not live account/provider or professional legal sign-off.

Before hosted activation, arrange restricted accounting/support and independent operational restore-journal custody, verified provider recovery/deletion procedures and sufficient monitored purge capacity to meet seven days. Support mailbox/export deletion is an operational obligation outside this application; no mailbox integration or independent personal-data backup is claimed. Existing nonpersonal question evidence archives are separate. Hosted migration, cron, payments, settings and websites remain untouched.
