Security responsibilities

Keep the Google folder restricted, use only the dedicated application's approved per-file permission, and store OAuth credentials and recovery keys through the secure operational process. Never put keys or tokens in chat, public pages, source control, client builds or ordinary logs. Preserve a separately secured recovery copy and verify retrieval.

Recovery and retention

Use approved operations and retention rules. Preserve legal holds, accounting dependencies, verified settlements, immutable closure decisions and restore suppression. An interrupted operation must be reconciled or safely resumed before access is restored. Independent storage delivery is not atomic with database writes.

Do not treat a redirect, successful upload or provider acknowledgement alone as proof of a recoverable backup. Verify the independent download, checksum, decryption and authority boundary before relying on a recovery point.
