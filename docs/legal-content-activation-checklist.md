# Legal content activation checklist

Date: 31 August 2026

The current legal routes are draft, visibly unapproved and `noindex`. Production activation is blocked until all of these are approved and evidenced:

1. Controller legal identity.
2. Trading name.
3. Geographic and postal contact details where required.
4. Privacy contact address.
5. ICO registration or fee decision.
6. Final lawful-basis assessment.
7. Children’s Code applicability assessment.
8. Numeric minimum-audience decision.
9. DPIA decision.
10. Processor and international-transfer review.
11. Stripe privacy and processor review.
12. Final retention approval.
13. Public-result and challenge deletion verification.
14. Terms review.
15. Consumer-rights and refund review.
16. Community-reporting process.
17. Privacy-request operating procedure.
18. Trusted Sites ingress verification.
19. Production D1 and migrations.
20. Final accessibility and legal review.
21. Explicit deployment approval.

No legal entity, address, email, DPO, company number, ICO number, lawful basis, transfer mechanism, processor agreement or professional approval may be inferred from this repository. Missing configuration must remain visibly unapproved rather than publishing a placeholder as fact.
