## Proposed customer-facing notice

### Who is responsible

Ayodele Ayeni trading as Classes for Culture is responsible for the personal
information used by What’s Your Bride Price?. Our business postal address appears
in the seller/contact details on this website. Email team@classesforculture.com for
support, privacy requests or complaints. We aim to acknowledge enquiries within
two working days; unresolved complaints are escalated to Classes for Culture.
That acknowledgement target does not replace statutory response deadlines.

### What we use and why

The game uses your selected region, quiz edition, questions, answers and completed
result to provide play and scoring. Choosing a region does not tell us your
ethnicity, nationality or location; we do not infer those characteristics.
Functional browser storage supports quiz recovery, local scores, privacy choices,
wallet ownership and return from a requested payment. Necessary ownership proof is
held separately from public links. A wallet’s balance, free-play count, bonus and
purchase ledger prevent duplicate credits, lost purchased value and repeat charges.

For free play and wallet continuity we use the minimum information needed for the
service, relying on our legitimate interests in providing and securing it. You can
contact us to object or request help. Paid checkout, fulfilment, wallet credits,
Royal Reveal and purchase confirmation use contract and steps you request before a
contract. Applicable accounting records rely on legal obligations. Proportionate
security, fraud prevention, dispute handling and service reliability use documented
legitimate interests; legal claims and specific legal duties can require records
to be retained. Optional first-party analytics relies on consent and is off at
launch. Refusing optional analytics does not stop play or access to purchases.

We receive order references, product, original GBP price, verified payment currency
and amounts, payment status, consent and refund/dispute information. Stripe handles
payment-card details; we do not store full card numbers or security codes. An email
address used for a requested purchase confirmation is protected during delivery;
necessary confirmation/terms/consent and payment records are retained as described
below. Support correspondence includes what you choose to send us. Please do not
send passwords, recovery keys, bank details or identity documents in ordinary email.

Our hosting and security providers process technical request information, which
can include IP addresses, timestamps and browser/request details. We minimise what
we record and restrict operational access. We do not sell player information,
use it for targeted advertising, or create advertising profiles. Cultural quiz
scoring does not determine legal rights or make similarly significant decisions.

### Photographs, generated media and sharing

An optional photograph stays in browser memory; it is not uploaded to us. Generated
portraits, certificates and other downloaded media remain under your control on
your device. Local display names and photographs are not required for server
scoring. Public results, challenges and optional analytics remain off at launch.
Sharing or opening another service is a deliberate action; the service you choose
then handles the information under its own privacy terms. We cannot remove copies
someone else has already downloaded, shared or cached.

### Providers and international processing

Cloudflare provides the production application, database and technical security
services. Netlify continues to host the existing website, private test and separate
recovery-information pages; it does not host the new production game database.
Google Drive is the proposed location for independently encrypted recovery archives.
The production archive account must have the approved business processing agreement
before it receives customer records. Namecheap Private Email transports purchase
confirmations and handles correspondence. Stripe provides payments, acting as a
processor for some services and an independent controller for fraud, regulatory
and other purposes described in its own notice. Healthchecks.io receives operational
status checks and operator contact details; we do not send player/order data in
those checks. No advertising or social-media tracker is installed by this game.

These providers can process information outside the UK. Cloudflare’s published DPA
includes UK transfer provisions; Stripe describes its roles and transfers in its
DPA and privacy notice. The final Google business-account agreement and current
Namecheap UK transfer details must be recorded before this paragraph is published.
We do not claim that a UK database location means all processing stays in the UK.
Contact team@classesforculture.com for information about recipients and applicable
transfer safeguards. Provider notices are linked below.

### How long information is kept

Quiz recovery and anonymous browser credentials last up to24 hours. Local best
scores remain until cleared. A private photograph is held in memory for at most
30minutes; removing it or closing its flow clears that memory. Downloads are
controlled by your device. The Cookie and Local Storage Notice lists each
functional storage item and its individual clearing/expiry behaviour.

Completed attempts, answers and results have a90-day active lifetime from the
original completion. Sharing or publishing never restarts it. Expired records
become unavailable at their deadline; bounded non-financial purge operations target
permanent removal within seven days, subject to protected records and holds.
Bonus Cowries expire180 days after award. Purchased Cowries do not expire while
the wallet remains active. Wallet closure requires reconciled payments, no remaining
purchased value and no unresolved case/hold. Necessary ownership information is
minimised30 days after immutable closure. Clearing local browser data does not
wipe an accounting ledger, restore closed access or cancel a purchase.

Pending payment authority lasts30 minutes. Necessary accounting evidence is kept
through the applicable UK tax-record deadline: at least five years after the
31January filing deadline for the relevant tax year. Refund/dispute evidence is
kept for six years after final settlement, with annual necessity review and
applicable holds. Routine support/privacy case evidence is kept for12 months after
closure. Holds and unresolved financial cases can require longer protection;
unrelated gameplay is not retained merely because another record is held.

Encrypted operational backups and restore-suppression records have a maximum
30-day window. If data is restored, current deletion/closure/suppression authority
is checked before access is released. Necessary accounting and unresolved payment
proof follow their separate longer rules. The protected confirmation delivery
address is removed from the delivery record after confirmed delivery or cancellation;
correspondence or payment-provider records have their own applicable periods.
Unknown email outcomes are held for review rather than retried blindly. Refer to
the full retention schedule for record-specific triggers and exceptions.

### Your choices and rights

Privacy choices explains functional storage and optional analytics and can clear
only this game’s listed local data. Optional analytics can be refused or its
consent withdrawn without affecting play; withdrawal stops future collection.
Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, erasure,
restriction, portability, object to legitimate-interest processing, and withdraw
consent. Some financial/legal records cannot be deleted immediately. We explain
any relevant restriction and avoid deleting unrelated purchased value. Contact
team@classesforculture.com; ownership proof may be needed, but never email a raw
wallet recovery key or password. You can complain to the UK Information
Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk, including without contacting us first.

### Age and changes

Free play and purchases are for adults aged18 or over. An adult acknowledgement
is required for purchases; it is not verified age assurance. We do not collect dates
of birth or identity documents to infer age. We keep high-privacy defaults for all
visitors because an age statement alone does not prevent younger people reaching
the website. No public profile, advertising, location tracking or behavioural
advertising is needed to play. Contact us if a child has used the service so that
we can help safely. Material notice or optional-processing changes will be
communicated before they take effect and any necessary consent will be obtained.
