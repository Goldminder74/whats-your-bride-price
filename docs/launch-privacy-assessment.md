# Launch privacy assessment — 26 October 2026

Engineering record for controller review. **Not legal approval or a published
notice.** The seller, secure address, UK-only paid market,18+ policy, support
contacts and voluntary14-day refund policy are already settled. No new identity,
age-verification, advertising or analytics service is proposed.

## Purpose, necessity and balancing

| Processing | Purpose and proposed basis | Necessity and less intrusive alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Pseudonymous quiz edition/selection/answers/result and recovery credential hash | Requested free play and wallet continuity; legitimate interests | Server validation prevents forged awards and preserves a requested session. Client-only scoring would not safely authorise paid wallet value. No real name, birth date or inferred ethnicity is necessary. |
| Purchased wallet value, order, terms, actual consent, verified currency/totals and confirmation | Requested paid service and pre-contract steps; contract | The order reference connects Stripe to the correct purchaser/result; verified signatures and original-GBP amounts prevent misdelivery. No full card number or banking credential is collected by the game. |
| Reconciled settlement, complaints, holds and necessary accounting proof | Applicable legal duties and legal claims, recorded separately | Keep the minimum relationship needed to explain balances/refunds. A payment hold must not justify retaining unrelated expired gameplay indefinitely. |
| Bounded admission, replay prevention, authority journal, encrypted backup and suppression | Reliable service, fraud prevention and protecting purchased value; legitimate interests | Anonymous identifiers rather than a named account; private service bindings; finite clocks; independent encryption. A restored snapshot alone cannot override later refund, closure, hold or deletion authority. |
| Optional analytics | Consent | OFF at launch. No advertising, third-party tracking or profiling is needed for this service. |

People can reasonably expect a quiz to remember their requested game and a wallet
to preserve purchased value. They should not have to expect public disclosure of
their identity, an ethnicity inference, advertising use or indefinite gameplay
history. Region selection is a content choice, not an assertion about the player.
Optional local photographs and display names are not required for server scoring.
Downloaded portraits/certificates leave the controller's control; sharing is a
separate deliberate choice. The privacy notice must explain these distinctions.

Residual risks include a lost ownership credential, a shared device, correlation
through a paid order, accidental sharing, a compromised operator account, repeated
abuse and recovery from an old archive. Existing controls include hashed ownership
proof, private/no-store sensitive responses, separation of public links from
credentials, signed Stripe validation, accounting reconciliation, encrypted
archives, immutable clocks, current independent authority and access suppression.
No backup or key-custody guarantee is inferred from a passing local fixture.

Objections and rights requests go to the monitored team mailbox. A proportionate
ownership check is required before disclosing a wallet/order; never request raw
recovery keys, passwords, banking details or identity documents in ordinary email.
Record the request/deadline, locate only relevant records, apply current holds and
settlement rules, execute the existing authorised closure/erasure procedure and
record its suppression authority. Escalate uncertain ownership or legal retention
to the controller. The two-working-day acknowledgement target is separate from
the applicable statutory response deadline. This is an operator procedure, not an
automated deletion endpoint or a guarantee of successful identification.

## Children and likely access

The settled service policy is18+ for free play and purchases. A cultural quiz and
its shareable pictures can nevertheless attract younger visitors. There is no
measured audience/marketing evidence establishing that children are unlikely to
access it. An adult statement or unchecked purchase acknowledgement is not verified
age assurance. This assessment therefore **does not claim an exemption** from the
Children's Code or treat that policy as its own proof.

Recommended launch configuration retains high-privacy defaults for everyone:
no optional analytics, advertising, precise location, public server profiles,
public result/challenge activation or server photograph upload. No new birth-date
or identity-document collection is proposed merely to label users as adults.
Purchases require the settled adult acknowledgement and separate immediate-delivery
request; the generous refund policy remains. Support should restrict an affected
child's access safely, consider erasure/suppression and protect unrelated purchased
value, using only proportionate ownership information.

Remaining controller decision: approve this purpose/necessity/balancing record and
the residual-risk treatment with the final verified privacy notice. If intended
marketing or actual audience includes children, revisit the assessment and any
appropriate age assurance before changing that launch configuration. Engineering
cannot invent evidence about audience or a controller/legal declaration.
