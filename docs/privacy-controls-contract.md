# Privacy controls contract

Date: 31 August 2026

Status: local technical contract; legal wording remains unapproved.

Privacy choices is available from the site-wide footer on every public route. Required functionality and optional first-party analytics are separate. Analytics remains an affirmative opt-in: no analytics preference, identifier, session storage or transmission exists before Allow; Allow and Do not allow have equal prominence; rejection does not block the quiz; withdrawal immediately removes the raw analytics credential and stops later events. Marketing and advertising remain off and are not presented as choices.

The current notice version is `analytics-notice-v1`. An Allow or Do not allow decision persists for 180 days. An analytics-session credential lasts at most 24 hours and is removed by withdrawal or local clearing. Server consent records follow the existing Prompt 16 expiry, and raw version-1 events cannot exceed 30 days. A changed purpose or materially changed inventory requires a new notice version and fresh choice. Reliance on a UK statistical-purpose exception is not implemented and would require separate owner and professional review of purpose limitation, individual-level retention, aggregation, objection controls and UK GDPR lawful basis.

Clear my local data presents the exact scope, requires confirmation, removes only registered application keys and the controlled nomination prefix, releases in-memory photograph and generated-media state where the page owns it, announces completion, and returns gameplay to a safe initial state. It does not emit analytics or create a replacement identifier. Downloaded files and server-held results, challenges, orders and entitlements are not represented as deleted.

The preferences dialog supports keyboard operation, focus containment, Escape, focus restoration, accessible status announcements, 320-pixel layouts, 200 percent zoom and reduced motion. Review scenarios are compiled only when both the review build and the dedicated privacy-fixture gate are authorised; query parameters alone cannot enable them.
