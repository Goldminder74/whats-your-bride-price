# West Africa under-representation report

Candidate occurrence counts are a research-coverage measure, not population weights or cultural rankings.

## Lowest coverage

- **Guinea: 2.** A local cultural institution or community specialist should identify additional everyday, contemporary and hospitality topics before approval targets are set.
- **Guinea-Bissau: 2.** A local cultural institution or community specialist should identify additional everyday, contemporary and hospitality topics before approval targets are set.
- **Sierra Leone: 2.** A local cultural institution or community specialist should identify additional everyday, contemporary and hospitality topics before approval targets are set.

## Known collection gaps

- Everyday greetings and etiquette need language-specific sources and community review; unsupported translations were deliberately excluded.
- Well-supported proverb candidates remain a gap because translations and attribution require language specialists.
- Liberia, Sierra Leone and Guinea-Bissau need stronger national-institution sourcing beyond the authoritative museum and UNESCO records used here.
- Rural and urban contemporary life outside the named heritage practices needs locally authored evidence.
- Wedding and courtship coverage is intentionally limited to one Mauritanian zaffa candidate pending specialist review; no practice is generalized across West Africa.
