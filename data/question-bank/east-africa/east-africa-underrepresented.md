## Countries and communities not yet represented

- Comoros and South Sudan need reliable, publicly accessible cultural sources with sufficiently precise community scope. No question was invented to fill those gaps.
- Additional Eritrean, Djiboutian, Somali, Mauritian and Seychellois everyday-life material should come from local institutions and community reviewers.

## Known collection gaps

- Everyday greetings outside the carefully sourced Kiswahili examples need language-specific sources and fluent community review.
- Well-supported local proverbs remain limited because translation, attribution and context require language specialists.
- Contemporary urban life, popular arts and hospitality outside formal heritage programmes need more locally authored evidence.
- Wedding coverage is limited to one well-documented Mauritian practice and references inside other performance records; none is generalized across the region.
- Sacred, initiatory, healing and oral-law records need knowledge-holder review before any wording can progress.
