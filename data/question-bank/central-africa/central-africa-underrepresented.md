# Central Africa under-representation and classification report

Counts measure research coverage, not population, cultural importance or editorial priority.

## Classification decisions

- **Angola:** included because Mbanza Kongo, Sona and cross-border Lunda Cokwe histories fit the application's Central edition; Angola also has strong Southern African connections.
- **Cameroon:** included in this product edition while acknowledging common West/Central and Sahelian classifications. Cross-border Kapsiki/Higi material names Nigeria and is not duplicated from the West pack.
- **Chad:** included for the established Central edition, with Saharan and Sahelian connections stated precisely.
- **Burundi and Rwanda:** not duplicated here because the repository's East Africa pack already covers them, despite some Central Africa classifications.
- **São Tomé and Príncipe:** represented through Tchiloli; broader island food, language and everyday-life coverage needs more locally authored institutional sources.
- **Equatorial Guinea:** current coverage is limited to the state's UNESCO Tentative List. Tentative status is never described as inscription. Living Fang, Bubi, Ndowe, Annobonese and other community practices remain underrepresented pending locally led sources and review.

## Known gaps

- Central African Republic has strong Aka music and Sangha ecology coverage but needs locally authored sources on urban life, foodways, languages and communities beyond the south-west.
- Congo and DRC need more contemporary sources beyond rumba, protected landscapes and museum histories.
- Wedding coverage is deliberately narrow: two Kapsiki/Higi research candidates demonstrate change and variation; no marriage payment or ceremony is generalized across countries.
- Greetings and proverbs were omitted because translation, register and attribution require fluent community reviewers and stronger locally authored sources.
- Food coverage focuses on cross-border cassava and Gnetum variation. Household recipes and claims of national ownership were avoided.
